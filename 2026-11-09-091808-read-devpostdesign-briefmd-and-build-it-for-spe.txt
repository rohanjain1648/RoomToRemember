  Where I departed from the brief
  - Placing card height: it sits 10 cm below eye level, not 20. At 20 cm it
    landed on the bottom edge of the Specs view in the preview.
  - Test hook: LEAF can't move the camera, so the main script has one hook,
    setViewerOverride(), that the tests use to stand in for walking. I also
    moved the real preview camera next to the couch: its dot grew and the
    Hint/Reveal buttons appeared.
  - Floor lamp: its object sits on top of the shade, which is close to eye
    height.
  - LEAF version: I used 2.0.2, not the latest 4.0. Version 4 needs newer
    interaction and UI kits than this project uses, so it wouldn't compile.
    Upgrading the kits is your call.
